Semi-custom Orthotics : Modular System with 18 Options

Modular System with 18 Options

A modular semi-custom system that determines the most suitable design based on foot shape, arch height, depth, width, heel alignment, contact area, and flexibility. With 18 modules available, it offers tailored combinations for different purposes. Compared with standard Pre-mold orthotics, it delivers a more precise customised effect, while providing quicker turnaround and greater cost-effectiveness than full\custom orthotics. In clinics, the most suitable model and module can be selected instantly according to the patient's condition, arch type and... plantar pressure profile, ensuring optimal solutions for rehabilitation, sport, and everyday activities.

PATHO-LOGIC :
Clinically Validated Modules for Pathological Conditions

The patho-logic range includes modules designed to address the problems of metatarsalgia, talalgia, supination, pronation and pathologies caused by instability in the midfoot and hindfoot. These modules have been clinically validated by practitioners and laboratories.
