CAD & Insole Fabrication

Healsy’s CAD system is a specialized software–based platform that captures the podiatrist’s expertise with precision. Using the patient’s foot data, practitioners can design orthotic shapes and corrective features such as arch height, rear‐foot alignment, and pressure distribution zones tailored to each order and visualize them in 3D. This digital design workflow ensures high fidelity, consistent quality, and time efficiency, delivering results optimized for clinical needs.

Healsy Custom Orthotics are handcrafted by skilled technicians who precisely combine thermoformable materials with a variety of modules, multi layer constructions, and corrective elements. Each insole is custom designed based on the patient’s foot condition, intended activity, and shoe type delivering not just functional support but also clinical accuracy and optimized fit. The thermoforming process, engineered for exact fitting and functional integrity, maximizes both conformity to the foot and corrective efficacy, providing a high-quality insole solution that satisfies medical requirements and enhances sports performance alike.
